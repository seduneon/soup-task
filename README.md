Soup AI Engineer take-home (hard track): GRPO + LoRA on Qwen3-4B (non-thinking) with own layer streaming and
L2L, everything under 8 GB of GPU memory on nvidia-smi.

## Scope

- **Required:** own L2L + layer streaming, data streaming with deterministic resume, GRPO under the 8 GB cap,
  proof that the model trained, silent-failure analysis, SHIP / DON'T SHIP verdict.
- **Streaming:** regular streaming is the baseline; KV-aware dynamic residency is the research arm (A/B).
- **Optional:** DFlash rollouts (bonus A/B arm) and the merge.

## Part 1: prediction vs measurement

| | resident / streamed | GPU peak | step time | generation share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| committed before any GPU run (assumed hardware, GSM8K shape) | 20 / 16 | 7641 MiB | 111.8 s | 83 % |
| re-run with constants measured on the RTX 3070, Countdown shape (before training) | 25 / 11 | 7603 MiB | 22.2 s | 76 % |

The dynamic-residency arm was predicted at −11.5 % for the GSM8K shape; on Countdown the A/B measured +0.1 %
[−0.4, +0.8] and it was rejected. Details and decision rules: [task1_budget/README.md](task1_budget/README.md);
gaps explained in REPORT.md §1.
